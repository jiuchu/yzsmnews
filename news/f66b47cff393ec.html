周三，国际空间站上的研究日程以使用超声波扫描和血压检查的先进生物医学研究为重点。第75次长期太空任务团队还在为感恩节抵达的货运任务以及计划于下周初抵达的另一艘货运飞船做准备。

NASA 飞行工程师杰西卡·沃特金斯（Jessica Watkins）和卢克·德拉尼（Luke Delaney）再次开展一项高科技人体研究调查，使用EchoFinder-2超声波设备来扫描他们的内脏器官。EchoFinder-2 采用增强现实技术和超智能算法，协助宇航员进行器官检测，有望减少对地面医疗团队进行宇航员健康监测的依赖。

美国宇航局的飞行工程师Anil Menon和加拿大航天局（CSA）的飞行工程师Joshua Kutyrk合作，更换了“宁静号”舱段的生命支持硬件。该模块从空气中去除水分，将其冷凝成水，并进行处理以供回收利用。库特里克还在“希望”号实验舱内准备了两颗由大学生设计的立方体卫星，以便将其送入地球轨道，用于拍摄地球和监测空间天气。

空间站站长彼得·杜布罗夫和来自俄罗斯联邦航天局的飞行工程师安娜·基基娜轮流使用臂式血压计测量血压，腕带和指套，以监测太空飞行对心血管系统的影响。

杜布罗夫和基基娜还回顾了“进步 MS-24”货运飞船的对接程序，该飞船载有约三吨的食物、燃料和物资，计划于感恩节当天美国东部时间上午 7:11 对接。NASA+ 和其他多个平台将从美国东部时间凌晨 6:30 开始直播飞船的抵达过程。

飞行工程师谢尔盖·弗拉索夫特捷里亚特尼科夫周三的轮值工作是在“Nauka”科学舱中清洁通风系统。特捷里亚特尼科夫还输送了水，并清洁了空间站俄罗斯航天局舱段的水箱。

今年最后一项货运任务正进入倒计时，该任务将于下周从佛罗里达州卡纳维拉尔角太空军基地发射，为第75次长期驻留任务的宇航员补给物资。诺斯罗普·格鲁曼公司的天鹅座 XL 货运飞船的目标是搭乘SpaceX猎鹰9号火箭，不早于11月29日（星期日）上午10:47升空。天鹅座XL将运送新的研究材料，以探索癌细胞的运动、工程化肌肉组织、受DNA启发的粒子等，从而促进人类健康。

Crew Works Biomedical Research Day Before Thanksgiving Space Delivery

查看英文全文

Advanced biomedical research with ultrasound scans and blood pressure checks topped the research schedule aboard the International Space Station on Wednesday. Expedition 75 is also preparing for a cargo mission arriving on Thanksgiving Day and another scheduled for delivery early next week.

NASA flight engineers Jessica Watkins and Luke Delaney were back on a high-tech human research investigation using the EchoFinder-2 ultrasound device to scan their internal organs. EchoFinder-2 uses augmented reality and super intelligence algorithms to assist astronauts with organ detection potentially reducing reliance on medical teams on the ground for crew health monitoring.

Flight engineers Anil Menon of NASA and Joshua Kutyrk of CSA (Canadian Space Agency) partnered together replacing life support hardware in the Tranquility module that removes moisture from the air, condenses it into water, and processes it for recycling. Kutryk also prepared a pair of college student-designed CubeSats inside the Kibo laboratory module for deployment into Earth orbit to photograph the Earth and monitor space weather.

Station commander Pyotr Dubrov and flight engineer Anna Kikina from Roscosmos took turns measuring their blood pressure using arm, wrist, and finger cuffs to monitor spaceflight’s effect on the cardiovascular system.

Dubrov and Kikina also reviewed procedures for the docking of the Progress 97 cargo spacecraft, carrying about three tons of food, fuel, and supplies, scheduled for 7:11 a.m. EST on Thanksgiving Day. NASA+ and a variety of other platforms will stream the spacecraft’s arrival live beginning at 6:30 a.m.

Flight engineer Sergey Teteryatnikov spent his shift on Wednesday cleaning ventilation systems in the Nauka science module. Teteryatnikov also transferred water and cleaned water tanks in the space station’s Roscosmos segment.

The final cargo mission of the year is counting down to its launch from Florida’s Cape Canaveral Space Force Station to resupply the Expedition 75 crew next week. Northrop Grumman’s Cygnus XL is targeted to lift off atop a SpaceX Falcon 9 no earlier than 10:47 a.m. on Sunday, Nov. 29. Cygnus XL will deliver new research to explore cancer cell movement, engineered muscle tissues, DNA-inspired particles, and more to promote human health.
